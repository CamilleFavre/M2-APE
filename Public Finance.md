## Presentation

Public finance has arisen as a key issue in Europe, as far as reaching a sound budgetary position is a major requirement of the Stability and Growth Pact. This question is very concrete: the current debt burden, implying massive transfers to future generations, seems to be now a popular question in the public opinion. Monitoring public finance is consensual but the necessary analytic framework is heavily discussed, especially today in the wake of the Covid-crisis (reform of the framework in France in 2021, reform of the Stability and Growth Pact under discussions), when investment needs increase pressure on public finances (ageing, green and digital transitions : should we adapt the framework?) and while interest rates rebound after decades-long decrease. The purpose of this course is to give some theoretical, empirical and practical material to these questions, in line with recent developments of economic analysis in the field of public finance.
