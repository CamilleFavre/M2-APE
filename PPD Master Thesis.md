## Presentation

In the "Lectures" section, you will find the master theses from last year which obtained a grade equal or higher than 16/20. (updated 27/10/22)
We draw your attention on two points:
Now, here is some important information about the master thesis, its submission and the deadlines during the academic year.
The M2 masters thesis is carried out under the supervision of a research supervisor. Students are encouraged to contact an instructor working on topics of interest to the student and to discuss possible dissertation topics. Every year, instructors also propose topics that students may wish to write on.
The final choice of a masters dissertaion supervisor must take place, at the latest, in January of the M2 year. A masters dissertation proposal (1 page maximum), approved and signed by a research supervisor, must be submitted to the programme administration at the latest at the end of December.
All PPD instructors and PSE memebers can serve as research supervisors for the masters dissertation. If your subject justifies it and with the approval of the director of the programme, you may choose a supervisor from outside the programme. In this case the rapporteur that you will chose for the defense must be a teacher from the PPD course.
The masters dissertation counts for 18 ECTS.
IMPORTANT! The masters dissertation is graded on a scale of 0 to 20. The defense lasts roughly 45-60 minutes before a jury composed of the masters dissertation and a rapporteur.
After the defense, the defense jury proposes a grade for the dissertation that is transmitted to the masters office. However, the final grade is attributed and validated ONLY by the end of the year jury on the basis of the grade proposed by the defense jury, the dissertation, and,for grades of 15 and higher, the jury report.
The jury report for grades equal or higher than 15 is compulsory.
In order to enter the PSE-Doctorate following the Masters programme, the average grade of all the M2 courses must be at least 14/20 and the masters thesis grade must be at least 15/20.

## - - ------------------------------------------------- Deadline: December 21st 2022 ------------------------------------------------

The thesis research project and your supervisor's name should be known before December 21st 2022.
The email with this information has to be sent by replying to this message, making sure the following people are in the loop:
- Both me and Homa
- Cc: your supervisor
Before sending us your research proposal, please ask the professors if they agree to be your supervisors and if they approve your research proposals.
It is your responsibility to make sure they are aware of the project you want to work on and approve it.
The document should include:
□ the title of your M2 thesis
□ the name of your supervisor
□ a brief description of what you are working on or a small introduction of your project with main references (1 page)
Please save your PDF under the following form:
[Last name] [First initial of your first name] – [title of the research project]
For example, for me, it would be: Devayres M. – this is my title
If you have trouble finding a subject to work on, don’t hesitate to go the master program directors’ office hours. By talking to them, you might be able to find ideas to help you decide.
The supervisor of your M2 thesis needs to be from PSE (teachers in the master, professor or research affiliate, see exact list on PSE website at https://www.parisschoolofeconomics.eu/en/about/academic-staff/).
If you have trouble finding a supervisor do not hesitate to talk to your professors during the research seminars.
Even if they do not deal exactly with the theme that you are interested in, they could direct you to someone else who could help you. This applies even if you still don't have a precise idea of what you wish to talk about.
Do not wait until the last minute.
