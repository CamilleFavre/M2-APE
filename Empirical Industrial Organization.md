## Presentation

Modern economies are characterized by ubiquitous, large and persistent forms of heterogeneity in any dimensions of business firms characteristics and of their dynamics. The primary scope of this course is to present an overview of the empirical investigations on the nature and on the evolution of such a rich landscape underlying industrial structures with a specific emphasis on developed economies. Simple stylized models able to generate the observed features of industrial dynamics are also presented and critically reviewed.
