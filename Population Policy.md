Population and policies Syllabus (2025-2026)
This course focuses on the intersection of demography and family economics, forming the foundation of the "Population Policies" course. It is highly recommended for students interested in specializing in population studies or broadening their perspective by exploring a complementary field. Key topics such as fertility, marriage, divorce, early childhood inequalities, and longevity have become increasingly central to economic research. The course does not require any formal prerequisites.

## Objectives

The course aims to provide students with fundamental demographic tools across various domains. It explores family economics and public policies in the context of population studies, focusing on developed countries. Drawing from both theoretical and empirical literature, the course examines the determinants and consequences of demographic behaviors, emphasizing the role of public policies and gender inequalities.
