## Presentation

This course focuses on multinational firms in the global economy: why they arise, their pattern of activity, and consequences for domestic economies. We will touch upon topics like international outsourcing (“offshoring”), organization of production, impacts on income distribution, “global value chains”. Readings are provided here, while additional readings will be provided in course slides.
All relevant information can be found in the syllabus.
