## Presentation

Syllabus of Katheline Schubert's part (24 hours)
I – Non-renewable resources Scarcity: facts and data The optimal extraction of a non-renewable resource The role of market structure Extensions: exploration and resource discoveries; technical progress in extraction; the Green Paradox; the taxation of non-renewable resources (Ramsey and strategic taxation) Fossil fuels and climate change
II – The energy transition, an economic perspective The Net-Zero objective and how to reach it Decarbonizing energy Renewables energies: costs, management of intermittency Climate policy
III – Growth with non-renewable resources The physical limits to growth The Dasgupta–Heal–Solow canonical model Social welfare and intergenerational equity Technical progress; backstop technology Endogenous growth
IV – Climate macroeconomics (1): the macroeconomic impacts of climate policy Direct benefits (avoided damages) Impacts on demand Stranded assets, stranded jobs
V – Climate macroeconomics (2): the tools Long run: Integrated Assessment Models Medium run: evaluation/simulation models Short run: RBC and E-DSGE
