## News

In Spring, every student presents twice. The first presentation can be short, on the research question, its motivation, the originality of the proposed thesis work, the data and empirical specification (for an empirical paper) and the state of the modeling so far (for a theoretical paper). The idea is to help you get started on the work and bring up any difficulties you may have encountered.
At the second presentation you should - if all works out - be able to present some empirical results, or a more advanced stage of your model (for those who do theoretical work).
