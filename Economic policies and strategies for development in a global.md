## Presentation

General Ambition of the course
This course covers policy aspects of contemporary economic development and te pursuit of poverty reduction in developing countries, with emphasis on the advantages and constraints arising from development taking place in a globalized world. Policies covered include trade, industrial policy, capital movements and debt, foreign aid, education, health and social policies. Special attention is also given to governance at both the domestic and global level, as well as to the distributional impact of policies. Each chapter of the course consists of: a) empirical facts about the issues addressed by policies; b) simple theoretical models that ground the reflection on those policies; and c) illustrations taken from the recent history of various developing and emerging countries.
