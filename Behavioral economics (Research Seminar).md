These seminars are for APE and PPD students interested in writing a masters' dissertation with advisors from the research group “Economics of Human Behaviour”. Please go to our webpage for more information on our research.
Note that:
(1) the group covers all topics, with a preference for studies that focus on the role of preferences and/or mechanisms (behavioural, social, institutional etc.)
(2) the group covers all quantitative methodological approaches: reduced-form or structural econometrics, causal analysis, experimental economics, theory, computational economics etc.
(3) most of us work at the intersection of several disciplines: economics and... cognitive sciences, psychology or sociology.
Organization of sessions
Students must choose which set of sessions they will register with before the first session (TO BE ANNOUNCED).
Objectives: to help students how to read in depth a research paper and how to elaborate a consistent research project.
Goal: students should have a research proposal and a supervisor by Christmas.
1. November-December: Each registered student gives a presentation of a key research paper during 40 mn (including Q&A); the paper is chosen jointly by the student (based on his/her interest) under guidance of her potential supervisor, or if the student has not yet chosen her supervisor, under our guidance.2. March-April: Each student presents for 40 mn his/her research question (theory, existing literature/testable hypotheses) / strategy (data, empirical issues) / overall pace of her project (milestones, potential threats, critical tasks).3. In the second semester (May at the latest), the students who wish, and all of the students who intend to apply for the PSE PhD program, will present their research at a mock defence (about one hour).
All research seminars are scheduled on Thursday afternoon to avoid all conflict with courses. We will send you more details after knowing the composition of the group. The grade for the research seminar will be identical to the grade obtained for the master dissertation. But very poor attendance / participation will be communicated to the jury.
