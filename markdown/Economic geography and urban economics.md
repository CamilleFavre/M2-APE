## Presentation

This course will provide an oversight of two distinct yet closely related fields of Economics - Urban economics and Land Policies - exploring the functionning of housing and land markets in an urban context.
The course will present the main theoretical contributions to each field, as well as seminal and recent empirical research in developed and developing countries. Current policy issues related to urbanisation, housing and land will also be discussed. The course will also draw on a walk in a neighbourhood of Paris to illustrate how the theories and concepts of Urban economics shape the urban space.
An introductory lecture will be dedicated to the measurement of urban phenomena and the difficulties encountered, and will provide a broief overview of the relationship between cities and economic development.
Three lectures will cover the field of Urban economics, presenting the monocentric urban land market model (Alsonso-Muth-Mills 1969, hedonic price analysis) and discussing issues of urban transport policies, housing markets and segregation.
Three lectures will cover the field of Land policies and environmental challenges associated with urbanization.
Grading of the course will be based on (i) participation in class and (ii) an exam.
