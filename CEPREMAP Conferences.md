## Presentation

[10/10/2024)
Coordinator: Eric Maurin and Akiko Suwa-Eisenmann
The CEPREMAP conferences are given by (i) economists working in French or international administrations/organizations, or (ii) scholars, often outside economics, whose work has strong policy implications. Each conference allows ample time for interaction and discussion, on the theme of the conference and on the institution where the speaker works.
This course is compulsory for PPD M1 students (no need to register). Students who enter directly in PPD M2 are welcome but need to register
Validation:
Validation is Pass or Fail, based on attendance and participation.Attendance : attendance to each conference is mandatory. Students are asked to arrive on time (no late arrival) and stay during the whole 2 hours session.
Participation: All students are asked to prepare at least two questions per session and ask them.
Each conference is taken charge by a group of 2-3 students. The group needs to:
1. prepare the topic of the conference. Students might (politely) contact the speaker for directions and references. However, speakers are high profile and very busy; they might not always reply to your emails.
2. write a document and send it two working days before the conference to the coordinators and the master office (akiko.suwa@psemail.eu; eric.maurin@psemail.eu; master.office@psemail.eu). The document file's name must be name student1_name student2_name of the presenter.pdf.
The document includes :
3. Lead the discussion on the day of the conference: introduce the speaker and the topic for about 6-8 minutes (2-3 minutes each).
4. Ask questions and make sure that everyone in the class participates.
Please set the groups before October 15, 2024 (between 3 and 4; avoid being a lone speaker). Write your name in the online document :
PPD conference cepremap schedule 2024-2025
Note regarding plagiarism:
Plagiarism is totally forbidden. However it has been regularly detected in previous years. Your contributions will be checked for copied and pasted sentences, and any detected plagiarism will result in failing the course, and may have more severe consequences. This being said, it is perfectly understandable that you draw on external sources; you need to properly cite them and give the references
Note regarding attendance:
Attendance is mandatory. If you cannot come to a lecture, you need to tell the admin and the organizers beforehand. Cheating regarding attendance (i.e. signing the presence sheet for someone else or having someone else signing for you) will have severe consequences.
